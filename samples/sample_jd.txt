Job Title: Business Intelligence Analyst

About the role:
We are looking for a Business Intelligence Analyst to join our data team and help
turn raw data into actionable insights for stakeholders across the company.

Responsibilities:
- Build and maintain dashboards and reports using Power BI and DAX
- Write complex SQL queries against our data warehouse (Snowflake)
- Partner with stakeholders to define KPIs and reporting requirements
- Automate recurring reporting workflows using Python
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders

Requirements:
- 1-3 years of experience in a BI, data analyst, or analytics role
- Strong SQL and Power BI / DAX skills
- Proficiency in Python for data automation
- Experience with Snowflake or another cloud data warehouse
- Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills
- Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field

Nice to have:
- Experience with Tableau
- Familiarity with AWS or Azure
- Exposure to machine learning concepts
